# Vice Verano: Afterglow

Vice Verano is my original browser crime game: a warm coastal city where Rico Salaz, a former wheelman, is trying to build a new life while Nadia Voss and Tito Bravo keep finding profitable complications. Coral Row, Harborline, Miraflores, Little Sombra and the Causeway are made at runtime from simple geometry, with their own colors, names and story.

## Playing

Start a new game from the title screen. Walk with `WASD`, sprint with `Shift`, jump with `Space`, and look around with the mouse. Walk up to a parked or moving car and press `F` to take it; while driving, `WASD` steers, `Space` handbrakes, and `F` gets Rico out. Hold the right mouse button to aim and click the left button to fire. `M` brings up the phone and `Escape` pauses.

The six missions move from a Coral Row car lift to a Causeway delivery, shop collections, a Harborline chase, a pier shootout and Marlow’s four-star escape. The gold diamond is the current objective. Car theft, collisions and gunfire bring police attention; break line of sight long enough and the heat cools. Continue uses the browser’s local save to restore money, health, position, wanted level and mission progress.

The art, vehicles, effects, music and sound effects are generated by the game itself. It uses no network assets, external fonts, real brands or borrowed story material.

## Local run

From the repository root, run:

```sh
node serve.ts --port 8081
```

Then browse to `http://localhost:8081/`.
